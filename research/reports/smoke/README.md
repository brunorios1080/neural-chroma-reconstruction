# Procedural audit report

This directory is the deterministic local audit generated by
`research/configs/benchmark_smoke.json`. It contains:

- 1,024 method-image-condition rows in `per_image.jsonl`;
- aggregate, paired, resource, codec-size, and provenance data in `report.json`;
- the exact configuration snapshot; and
- 32 automatically selected difficult-boundary sheets plus `qualitative/index.json`.

The four held-out inputs are procedurally generated PNGs. All 12 synthetic 4:2:0
siting/filter conditions, JPEG qualities 30/50/70/90, H.264 and HEVC CRF
18/23/28/35, and AV1 CRF 20/30/40/50 completed with a local FFmpeg build. The
report records the resolved executable, available encoders, exact per-row
command, encoded size, and zero skips.

These files prove that the local protocol is executable. They are not a
natural-image benchmark and none of their quality scores should be cited as
evidence for a method.
